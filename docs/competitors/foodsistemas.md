# Food Sistemas (foodsistemas.com.br): what to learn

A Brazilian all-in-one system for restaurants, pitched as "the whole restaurant in one system".
Studied 2026-11-26 (home, prices, segment and comparison pages, product screenshots).

## How they sell

- **One product, 22 segment pages.** Restaurant, pizzeria, burger bar, bakery, açaí, food truck,
  sushi and more. Each page has its own headline and a feature list in that trade's words (pizza
  with 2–4 flavours, stuffed crusts, P–GG sizes, "pause a flavour that ran out"), but it is the same
  system configured differently. This is exactly our model (templates per business type).
- **One price, everything included.** R$890/month: no setup fee, no lock-in, no paid add-on modules.
  "Every new feature any customer asks for goes to all customers."
- **"The restaurant doesn't adapt to the system, the system adapts to the restaurant."** Live in 2
  hours; they import your products, photos and data; customisation the same day; "if it doesn't do
  it yet, we build it" — today. One App's AI makes this promise scalable without people.
- **Trust signals up front.** Google rating, human support around the clock on WhatsApp, 30 days
  free, customer logos, real screenshots from their own demo store.
- **"Generic systems fail everyone" comparison page.** Rigid flows, support queues, lock-in.

## How the product looks (why ours feels dull next to it)

| Pattern | What they do | One App today |
|---|---|---|
| **Purpose-built operational screens** | Table map (big tiles: dark = occupied with total and time, white = free, amber = part paid), point-of-sale with product photos, kitchen screen, delivery board | Generic table, list and board blocks |
| **Colour means something** | Board columns with full-colour headers (red Accept, amber Preparing, green Ready, blue On the way); timers turn amber and red as orders wait; channel badges (iFood, 99, WhatsApp) | Grey columns with a small dot |
| **Action buttons on cards** | "Accept / Reject", "Start preparing", "Mark ready", "Collect R$ 297,90" right on the card: the next step is one tap | Drag or open the record |
| **Photos everywhere** | Product photos in the till and on the menu, the business's logo on the dashboard and headers | Photos only inside records |
| **Big numbers with context** | KPI cards with icon, colour, sub-line ("52 cancelled") and comparison with the previous period; "Good morning, [logo] [business], [date]" header | Plain number tiles |
| **Counts in the navigation** | Tabs "Tables 28 · Orders 75 · Delivery 13 · 4 TO COLLECT" | No counts |
| **Dense but clear** | Lots of information per card, strong typography, generous colour, dark top bar | Light, spacious, low contrast |

## What we take from this

1. **Visual blocks for every business** (platform):
   - board columns with coloured headers;
   - cards with a photo, an accent colour and a next-step button;
   - KPI tiles with icon, tone and comparison;
   - a gallery block (photo cards);
   - a "Good morning" header with the business logo and today's numbers;
   - counts in the menu.
2. **Operational screens per type**, built as designed pages on those blocks: a bay map for
   mechanics (like their table map), a chair or day view for salons, a kitchen or order screen for
   food.
3. **Segment landing pages + demo per type** (marketing): `/for/mechanics`, `/for/hair-salons`,
   each with the trade's words, its screens and "Try the demo".
4. **Same-day promise, by AI**: "describe what you need and it's there today". Import their data
   (CSV/Square already exist) as part of onboarding.
5. **One simple price, everything included, no lock-in.**
