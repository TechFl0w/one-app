# Product

## Promise
"Set up a whole management system for your business in a few prompts — then change anything by asking."

## Who it's for
1. Small businesses using **nothing** yet (paper, Instagram DMs, texts, Google Calendar) — primary target.
2. Businesses on Fresha / Square / Vagaro who want one tool — won with an **import tool**, not long-term sync.

## Core experience
- Onboarding interview (3–6 questions, multiple choice where possible) → template → "Here's what I'll build"
  preview → **Build it**.
- Owner changes anything by clicking (Edit mode) or asking the AI: rename menus/fields, add fields, new pages
  and boards, rearrange layouts, add automations.
- Customers reach the business on its own WhatsApp number (later phase) handled by a limited customer agent.

## Reference examples
- **Ornament maker:** Products/stock, Orders, Customers; Etsy import; shipping labels; WhatsApp order intake
  with owner approval; low-stock alerts; Christmas ship-by cut-off.
- **Hair salon, chair rental:** Chairs, Renters, Agreements, Rent payments, Documents (licence/insurance with
  expiry); applicant pipeline; e-signature; weekly auto-charge; expiry reminders; public "Rent a chair" page.
  Agreement template must be approved by the owner (not legal advice).
- **Nail salon (bookings):** practitioners, stations, services with duration/buffers/add-ons, deposits,
  reminders, rebooking nudges. Calendar by practitioner or by station.
- **Mechanic:** drop-off/capacity booking, bay board, quote → WhatsApp approval → work, vehicle history.

## Business types by pattern (template roadmap)
A. 1:1 exact-time — salons, barbers, lashes, spas, massage, physio, dentists, tattoo, trainers, tutors, groomers
B. Drop-off / capacity — mechanics, tire shops, detailing, repair shops, tailors, dry cleaners, day care
C. On-site / mobile — cleaners, trades, mobile services, landscapers, movers
D. Group / classes — yoga, gyms, martial arts, workshops, camps
E. Space & equipment rental — chair/booth, desks, studios, courts, equipment, storage
F. Hospitality slots — escape rooms, tours (restaurants: low priority)

## Not in v1
Owner WhatsApp line, in-person card readers, template marketplace, custom code steps, long-term two-way sync.
